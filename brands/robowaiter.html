Brand story

Supplement your team — don’t replace hospitality.

A robot waiter delivers food to diners and can take away dirty dishes. Staff still load orders, set destinations and keep the guest experience human. RoboWaiter positions robots as a cost-effective answer to labour pressure — freeing people to focus on guests.

Smart Tech Solutions is a Melbourne smart-technology company founded in 2017 by IT professionals. They sell and install serving robots for restaurants, cafés, entertainment venues and aged care across Australia.

Features RoboWaiter highlights

What the brand puts front and centre.

4 adjustable trays

Flexible tray heights for different plate stacks — single tray load class around 10 kg for restaurant service runs.

Intelligent obstacle avoidance

Lidar, depth vision, stereo RGBD, collision sensors and infrared ranging for omnidirectional sensing on busy floors.

Smart expressions

Expressive faces and Android-based interaction for guest-facing moments that feel warmer than a plain cart.

Long battery life

Working time over 15 hours and standby over 48 hours — suited to long service days and event venues.

Lease / rent to buy

Published A$39/day lease with own-at-end pathways (24-month contract or buy after 12 months) as listed by RoboWaiter.
