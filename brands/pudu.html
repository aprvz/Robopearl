Brand story

Easier work. Better service. One brain, many embodiments.

Pudu’s mission is to empower easier work and better lives through AI and robotics. The company holds full-stack proprietary R&D across navigation, multi-robot scheduling, swarm control and motion — powering a portfolio from specialised delivery robots to cleaning and industrial platforms.

Flagship hospitality models such as BellaBot (launched 2019) and BellaBot Pro are deployed by global brands including Pizza Hut, Jollibee, Carrefour, KFC and Walmart for food running, greeting and on-floor advertising.

How Pudu explains the product

Core capabilities Australian venues rely on.

Embodied navigation

VSLAM, lidar and marker fusion for reliable paths through busy dining rooms, hotel corridors and multi-floor facilities — with tight path clearance for Australian floor plans.

Guest interaction

Touch screens, expressions, voice and advertising displays turn delivery into a branded moment — BellaBot Pro’s dual LCDs and KettyBot Pro’s greeter screen lead the hospitality line.

Multi-robot scheduling

Fleet coordination so several units can share a venue without colliding on routes — useful for groups running food delivery and dish return together.

Restaurant workflows

Food running, drinks, dish return (HolaBot) and covered premium delivery (SwiftBot) map to real FOH and BOH jobs, not just showroom demos.

Hotel & facility runs

FlashBot Max adds enclosed multi-floor delivery with lift and access integration for amenity and room-service logistics.

Global proof, local supply

Shipped at global scale; Robopearl surfaces Australian seller AUD cues and handles demo, mapping and training enquiries for your site.

Flagship experience

BellaBot Pro — delivery, advertising and presence.

PUDU’s next-generation catering and retail service robot adds AI interaction, dish identification, curved advertising display and stronger safety features — built on years of BellaBot deployments worldwide.

  • 40 kg payload across multi-tray service
  • Dual LCD screens for interaction and promotions
  • ~65 cm path clearance for tight dining rooms
  • About 11 hours runtime; food, drinks and bussing modes
